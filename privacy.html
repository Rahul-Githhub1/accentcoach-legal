Privacy Policy

My Accent Coach · effective 18 November 2026

My Accent Coach is an accent and pronunciation practice app for iPhone and iPad, published by Rahul Parkale. It is built so that your practice stays yours: there is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no developer server.

In short: your recordings stay on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, the text and scores of your practice are stored in your own private iCloud account. Audio is never uploaded, and nobody but you can read the iCloud copy.

What stays on your device

  • Recordings. Your voice is saved as audio files inside the app on this device. They are never uploaded to us or to a third party.
  • Practice history. Lesson names, scores, dates and word-level detail are stored locally.
  • Settings. Your voice choice, speed, reminder time and other preferences are stored locally.
  • Usage counts. Off by default. If you turn counts on, the app keeps counts only — app opens, sessions, lines scored, lessons finished and share taps, by day. It stores no audio, transcript, lesson text, score, device identifier or account. Nothing is uploaded automatically; you can export the counts as a JSON file if you choose to.

Speech recognition

The app uses Apple's Speech framework to work out which words it heard. Where your device and language support on-device recognition, that happens on the device. Where they do not, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive a copy of your audio from Apple, and the app does not send it to us.

iCloud history sync (off by default)

If you turn on Sync history with iCloud, the app stores the non-audio part of each take in your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. That copy contains the lesson title, score, date and the words that were missed, close or uncertain. It contains no audio, no recording file name and nothing else about your device.

We cannot read your private iCloud database. Apple hosts and protects it under your Apple ID and the iCloud terms. Turning the setting off stops syncing immediately but leaves the existing iCloud copy in place, so you can turn it back on without re-uploading. To remove the iCloud copy, delete the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or delete the app and its iCloud data. Deleting the app from a device removes the local history and recordings on that device; other devices are unaffected.

Sharing

The progress share card and the usage-counts export open the system share sheet. Nothing leaves the app unless you pick a destination and confirm it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in to the app.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier and no tracking across apps or sites.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No server of ours that receives your practice.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is intended for general audiences. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Keep iCloud sync off (the default) to keep everything local.
  • Keep usage counts off (the default) so nothing is counted.
  • Delete individual recordings from the app, or delete the app to remove its local data.
  • Remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings as described above.
  • Control microphone and speech recognition permission in iOS Settings → My Accent Coach.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. A change that affects what leaves your device will also be noted in the app's release notes.
