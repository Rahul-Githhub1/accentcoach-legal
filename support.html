My Accent Coach · iPhone and iPad

My Accent Coach helps you practise an accent line by line: listen to a native model, record yourself, and get a score for what you said. This page answers the questions that come up most often. If yours is not here, email me.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and allow microphone and speech recognition access when asked. Both are needed to score a take.
  2. Pick a lesson, then a line.
  3. Tap Listen to hear the model pronunciation.
  4. Tap Record, say the line, then tap Stop. You get a score and word-level feedback straight away.

Common questions

I get no score, or "Couldn't hear that"

  • Check that the microphone is not blocked: iOS Settings → My Accent Coach → Microphone.
  • Record somewhere quieter. Background noise, music and fans make recognition less accurate.
  • Speak a little closer to the device and at a normal pace. Shouting or rushing lowers the score.
  • Make sure the language you are practising matches the language set in the app.

Scoring stopped working after I took a phone call

The app releases the microphone when another app needs it, and picks it back up when the call ends. If it does not recover, close the app from the app switcher and open it again. Music that was playing before you started a take should resume by itself when the take ends.

My Bluetooth headphones or AirPods behave oddly

If you take the headphones off or they disconnect mid-take, the take ends rather than recording silence. Reconnect them and start a new take. Switching output back to the speaker is handled automatically — you should not need to restart the app.

Where are my recordings kept?

On your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded. Deleting a take in History deletes its audio from the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What is "Sync history with iCloud"?

An optional setting, off by default. When on, the text and scores of your takes are copied to your own private iCloud account so your history follows you to your other devices. Audio is never included, and I cannot read your iCloud data. Turning it off stops syncing but keeps the existing copy. See the privacy policy for how to remove the iCloud copy.

I have two devices and my history looks different on each

With iCloud sync on, history merges rather than overwrites: if the same take exists on both devices, you keep it, and no take is ever deleted by syncing. If one device still looks out of date, open the app on both while online and give it a moment. Sync status is shown in Settings.

Sync shows an error

Check that you are signed in to iCloud (iOS Settings → your name) and online. If the message mentions CloudKit or an operation code, send it to me — that detail is what I need to diagnose it.

How do I change the voice, speed or reminder?

All of that lives in Settings inside the app: voice, playback speed, daily reminder time, and whether usage counts are on. Usage counts are off by default and store only numbers — never audio, text or scores.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Lessons, recording and scoring work without a connection. Only iCloud sync needs the network.

Is there an Android or web version?

Not at the moment. My Accent Coach is iPhone and iPad only.

Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Email customersupport@bitlayer.site with:

  • your device model and iOS version;
  • a screenshot if the problem is visual.

Accessibility

The app supports Dynamic Type and VoiceOver. If something is hard to read, reach or hear, tell me — accessibility reports get priority.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. Recordings stay on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
